NSTB

The NSTB is the consultative body of the student gymnastic associations in the Netherlands. Even though the NSTB has been active for a long time now it only became an official association in 2008. All documents that can be downloaded directly are on a separate page: documents. On that page, the official articles of the association can be found, as well as the house regulations and various manuals.
