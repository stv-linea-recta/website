External Trainings

Every month, there is an external training in a gymnastics hall in Hengelo, Almelo or Rijssen. Here we can train using a big square floor, foam pits, and many other things!

For transport to the training we usually go by car or public transport (to Hengelo we go by bike). Travel expenses can be invoiced to Linea Recta, so you can enjoy the external trainings without extra costs!
