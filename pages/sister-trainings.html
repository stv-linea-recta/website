Sister Trainings

Some of our sister associations organise trainings that all NSTB associations are allowed to join. U-turn (Utrecht) and KUNST (Nijmegen) have been doing so regularly for the past few years. Below you can read some more about the individual associations.

U-turn training

Our sister association U-turn has an open training every third sunday of the month. In Utrecht, they have a good gymnastics hall with all the apparatuses, a big foam pit and a square floor. Ideal to try out new elements or prepare for a competition!

KUNST training

Our sister association KUNST from Nijmegen organises open trainings from time to time, but less regularly. They have a well-equipped gymnastics hall, including a square floor, foam pit, and anything else you may need.
